✱ ABOUT ME

I am Anthony Lam. A photographer and graphic designer. A creative director and Digital Media Art student at San Jose State University. I have had a camera in my hand since I was twelve. Documenting everything from skateboarding with friends to cars in a parking lot to countless parties. What started as capturing moments with friends became part of my identity as a storyteller through visuals. My work captures everyday moments and the feeling of fleeting youth. I am drawn to the imperfections. Moments where grit and magic meet. Working across photography and video as well as design and creative direction. I aim to create work that feels immediate and expressive. Distinctly human in an increasingly automated world where imperfection is so often edited away.

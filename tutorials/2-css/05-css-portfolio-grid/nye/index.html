NEW YEARS EVE PARTY IN NYC

✱ READ MORE

Photographed on Fujifilm. This series captures New Year's Eve in Brooklyn with The Dare. The warehouse felt like a steel microwave. Packed with hot and sweaty people dancing feverishly to welcome the new year. I wanted to capture the chaos of the night. The movement. The feeling of fleeting youth that filled the room. Strangers pressed together as the hours disappeared. A moment that felt endless while it was happening.

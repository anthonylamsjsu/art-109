✣ ABOUT ME ✣

I am Anthony Lam, a photographer, graphic designer, creative director, and Digital Media Art student at San Jose State University. I have had a camera in my hand since I was twelve, documenting everything from skateboarding with friends to cars and parties. What started as capturing moments with friends became part of my identity and a way to tell stories through visuals. My work captures everyday moments and the feeling of fleeting youth. I am drawn to the imperfections, moments where grit and magic meet. Working across photography, video, design, and creative direction, I aim to create work that feels immediate, expressive, and distinctly human in an increasingly automated world where imperfection is so often edited away.

NEW YEARS EVE PARTY IN NYC

Photographed on a Fujifilm disposable camera, this series captures New Year's Eve in Brooklyn, New York, with The Dare. The warehouse felt like a steel microwave, packed with hot, sweaty people dancing feverishly to welcome the new year. I wanted to capture the movement, chaos, and sense of fleeting youth that filled the room that night.

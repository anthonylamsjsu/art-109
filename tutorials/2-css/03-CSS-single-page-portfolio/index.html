✣ ABOUT ME ✣

I'm Anthony Lam, a photographer, graphic designer, and Digital Media Art student at San José State University. Since I was twelve, I've had a camera in my hand, whether recording my friends and me skateboarding or shooting montages of cars and parties. I don't believe creativity should be limited by rules or technical perfection. To me, the best work comes from imperfections, born out of grit and magic.

NEW YEARS EVE PARTY IN NYC

Photographed on a Fujifilm disposable camera, this series captures New Year's Eve in Brooklyn, New York, with The Dare. The warehouse felt like a steel microwave, packed with hot, sweaty people dancing feverishly to welcome the new year. I wanted to capture the movement, chaos, and sense of fleeting youth that filled the room that night.
